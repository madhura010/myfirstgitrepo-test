Aganitha Roles & Personas Framework

Small Molecule / Drug Design Dossier Guide

A multi-perspective evaluation guide for medicinal and synthetic chemists reviewing compound dossiers prior to initiating small molecule drug discovery synthesis.

📐
Architect
Structural & Pharmacophore Architecture
  • Core Scaffold & Vectors: Central heterocycle rigidity, exit vectors for SAR exploration, and core-substituent seams.
  • Physicochemical Space: Molecular Weight ($\text{MW} \le 500$), $\text{LogP}$ ($1 - 4$), $\text{TPSA} \le 140\ \text{\AA}^2$, and $\text{nRotB} \le 10$.
  • Fraction $\text{sp}^3$ ($f_{\text{sp3}}$): Target $f_{\text{sp3}} \ge 0.35$ to improve aqueous solubility and reduce planar aggregation.
  • Target Binding Mode: X-ray / Cryo-EM structural H-bonds, salt bridges, hydrophobic pocket filling, and halogen bonding.
Architect's View: "Is the core scaffold rigid enough to lock the active conformation while leaving clean exit vectors for SAR?"
🤝
Collaborator
Synthetic Strategy & SAR Exploration
  • Divergent Synthesis Route: Advanced intermediate design allowing rapid 20–50 analog parallel diversification.
  • Building Block Availability: Commercial sourcing of boronic acids, aryl halides, and amines with short lead times.
  • Robust Reaction Types: High-yielding transformations (Suzuki, Buchwald-Hartwig, Amide coupling, $S_N\text{Ar}$, Reductive Amination).
Collaborator's View: "Building on your key coupling step... let's design a core intermediate that lets us synthesize 30 diversely substituted analogs in 2 weeks."
🔍
Skeptic
ADME Liabilities, Toxicity & IP Risks
  • Metabolic Soft Spots: Unblocked $p$-positions on aromatic rings vulnerable to CYP oxidation; labile ester/amide linkages.
  • Reactive Metabolite Alerts: Structural alerts (anilines, catechols, thiophenes, nitroaromatics) that form GSH adducts.
  • hERG & Off-Target Liability: Basic amine ($\text{p}K_a > 8.5$) + lipophilic tail ($\text{LogP} > 3$) causing hERG channel binding ($IC_{50} < 10\ \mu\text{M}$).
  • PAINS & FTO: Pan-Assay Interference filters and patent freedom-to-operate (FTO) Markush claim clearance.
Skeptic's View: "Sub-nanomolar target potency is useless if your basic amine causes hERG cardiotoxicity and rapid CYP clearance."
✂️
Minimalist
Retrosynthetic Simplicity
  • Step Economy: Keep overall synthetic route to $\le 5 - 7$ linear steps from commercial starting materials.
  • Protecting Group Avoidance: Eliminate unnecessary Boc/Cbz/Bz protecting group steps through reordered reaction sequences.
  • Stereochemical Pruning: Avoid non-essential chiral centers; use chiral pool or enantiopure building blocks if required.
Minimalist's View: "Remove the two non-essential stereocenters and the 3-step protecting group sequence. What is the 4-step direct route?"
✅
Verifier
Analytical Proof & Data Integrity
  • Purity & Proof: $^{1}\text{H}/^{13}\text{C}$ NMR, HRMS, and HPLC purity ($>95\%$).
  • Enantiomeric Purity: Chiral HPLC / SFC confirming $\%ee > 98\%$.
  • In Vitro ADME Suite: Kinetic/thermodynamic solubility (pH 2.0 & 7.4), Caco-2 permeability ($P_{app}$), and liver microsomal $CL_{int}$.
Verifier's View: "Provide the HPLC 254nm trace (>95%), Chiral SFC trace, and human microsomal clearance $CL_{int}$ before signing off."
🥊
Mentor
Tough-Love Sparring Partner
  • Translational Reality Check: Potency in cell-free assays must translate to cellular activity and oral bioavailability ($F$).
  • Synthetic Viability: Don't spend 14 steps producing 5 mg of compound when an optimized 4-step analog delivers the same efficacy.
Mentor's View: "Enzyme potency without oral bioavailability ($F > 30\%$) is a dead end. Solve for ADME and route efficiency first!"
